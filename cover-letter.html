Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my interest in the Industrus Engineering Graduate Program.

I am a prospective graduate from the University of Technology Sydney, completing a Bachelor of Engineering majoring in Data Science. I recently completed a 12-week internship as a Data Engineer at Optik Consultancy, where I worked alongside a cross-disciplinary team of engineers to apply emerging technologies in developing a tailored, AI-powered solution for our client. I am highly interested in the opportunities this role affords for creating technical solutions for real-world industry problems. I believe my combination of technical knowledge, practical industry experience and passion for leveraging emerging technologies to create innovative solutions makes me well suited to this graduate role.

A commitment to ethical conduct and the highest standards of professional accountability (3.1)

During my internship I maintained a strong commitment to professional accountability, particularly during the project handover. At the project handover, I ensured that the software was effectively communicated to the client via documentation, a video user guide and a walkthrough meeting to allow our client to effectively understand and use the software we developed. Throughout development we also maintained open communication regarding project scope, the use of AI technologies and technical limitations or challenges that arose during development. By fostering this level of transparency and accountability, I was able to ensure that our client received a technically sound solution, along with sufficient documentation to enable further development and implementation.

Demonstrated ability to effectively communicate both with other engineers and with stakeholders from different fields (3.2)

During my internship, I worked closely with cross-disciplinary team of six engineers spanning software, civil and data engineering, as well as with our client; Damage Control Project Management (DCPM). As a civil infrastructure company, DCPM's expertise is primarily concerned with infrastructure repair and project management rather than software and AI technologies. We conducted regular meetings and interviews with our client to understand their current workflow, the challenges they were facing, and the outcomes they were hoping to achieve from our project. As a team member with a strong data engineering background, it was one of my key responsibilities to identify emerging technologies and assess their viability as solutions to address our client's needs. This required me to communicate complex technical concepts in an accessible manner, catering to our client's technical background. This effective communication allowed our team to develop a technical solution that remained aligned with our client's needs and expectations.

The ability to engage with a creative, innovative and proactive environment (3.3)

Road defect detection and measurement is a significant challenge in the road assessment field. Rather than applying traditional photogrammetry or orthographic camera-based methods, our team devised a novel solution combining homography, machine learning and computer vision to automatically identify and measure road defects. This innovative solution significantly reduced the amount of manual work involved in our client's road assessment workflow and demonstrates how emerging technologies can be applied in creative ways to solve practical engineering problems. I was primarily responsible for developing the homography and defect segmentation pipelines. This experience strengthened my ability to proactively seek innovative technical solutions rather than relying solely on established approaches. By applying this innovative thinking, I was able to develop a system that stands apart from any existing solutions on the market.

Demonstrated the ability to use and manage information (3.4)

During my internship I was required to interpret a large amount of information from our client, including their existing workflow, requirements, data and current software. Before developing a solution, I worked closely with our client to understand how their systems operated and to identify limitations. Using this information to guide technical decisions allowed us to craft a solution that was not only technically effective but also integrated smoothly into the client's existing workflow. This experience demonstrated my ability to manage and organise large volumes of data in order to make informed technical decisions.

The ability to manage your own performance in a professional environment (3.5)

Throughout my internship, I was responsible for a large portion of the machine learning components of the project, including automated segmentation and homography. To stay on track, I regularly reviewed my progress and maintained my tasks through our team's Trello board and participated in daily team check-ins to ensure I remained on schedule. Our team also met with our client weekly via Microsoft Teams, where we provided concise, detailed updates on project development and raised technical obstacles, questions or decisions requiring client input. As a result, I consistently delivered my sections on time and was able to keep both my team and client well informed throughout the project development, strengthening my ability to manage my performance independently in a professional setting.

A demonstrated ability to work as part of a team and to show leadership when required (3.6)

I have also developed my ability to work as part of a team and show leadership through my university project work. In Professional Studio A, I was designated as team leader for a software project involving the development of an AI-powered photo enhancement application. I worked with the team to divide roles according to individual skill sets, technical knowledge and areas of interest. We successfully developed a minimum viable product and achieved a High Distinction for the project. This experience demonstrated my ability to work effectively as part of an engineering team, and to recognise and leverage individual strengths to deliver an effective product.
