Internship at Optik Consultancy

Earlier this year I completed a twelve-week internship as a data engineer with Optik Consultancy, working within a cross-disciplinary team of six software, data and civil engineers. Our client, Damage Control Project Management (DCPM), needed a faster way to identify and measure road defects across large stretches of infrastructure.

We designed and built an automated measurement system combining homography, computer vision and machine learning to detect defects directly from field footage — replacing a slow, manual assessment process with something DCPM's team could run themselves. I was primarily responsible for the homography and defect-segmentation pipelines.
